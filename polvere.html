Polvere

some absences never disappear from the internet.

an interactive web-based artwork exploring digital identity, absence and the persistence of online traces. the project invites the viewer to navigate a fragmented digital space suspended in time. through forgotten messages, personal records, audiovisual components and an unreliable interface, a sense of an absent identity gradually emerges. what initially appears to be a familiar digital environment becomes an unsettling encounter with a presence that has never quite disappeared.
