Distante

trying to restore what memory has already changed

an exploration of the relationship between memory, time and perception, reflecting on how memories become altered as they recede into the distance - thus, becoming "distanti". originally conceived as a photographic work, the project evolves into an interactive installation in which the viewer attempts to restore a distorted image to its original form. however, just as distant memories become fragmented and transformed over time, the original remains ultimately unreachable.
